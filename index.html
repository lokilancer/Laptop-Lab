                  Docs

                  What works where?

                  Camera, microphone, keyboard and speakers work in all current browsers. Battery info and speaker selection need Chrome, Edge or Opera. Camera and microphone need HTTPS or localhost and your permission.

                  How do stamps work?

                  A stamp is awarded when a test shows real results, such as every screen fill viewed or both speaker channels played. Speaker and display stamps record that the test ran; you judge by eye and ear whether it looked and sounded right.

                  Why can't I change settings?

                  Browsers can read and test hardware but can't change system settings such as keys, brightness or power plans. Use Windows settings for those.
